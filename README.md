# 🚀 Customer Churn Prediction (Production-Level ML Project)

## 📌 Problem Statement

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses. Losing customers directly impacts revenue and growth.

This project builds a **machine learning model to predict customer churn** and helps businesses take **proactive retention actions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

* Identify customers likely to churn
* Reduce revenue loss by early intervention
* Provide actionable insights to business teams

---

## 💼 Real-World Impact

* Helps companies reduce churn rate
* Improves customer retention strategies
* Enables targeted marketing campaigns

---

## 🧠 Project Highlights (Resume Ready)

* Built an **end-to-end ML pipeline** for churn prediction
* Focused on **Recall optimization** to minimize customer loss
* Performed **feature engineering & data preprocessing**
* Compared multiple ML models to select best performer
* Generated **business insights from data**
* Designed project ready for **real-world deployment**

## 📈 Key Insights

* Customers with **low tenure** are more likely to churn
* **High monthly charges** increase churn probability
* **Contract type** strongly affects retention
* Customers using more services show **lower churn rate**
